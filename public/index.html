          The Draw

          Snake draft across 4 pots. Lower pot number = stronger seed. Beating a higher pot is worth +3.

          How it works

          The Draw

          48 teams are split into 4 quality pots. We snake-draft pot by pot — the player who picks last in pot 1 picks first in pot 2, and so on. The shuffle inside each pot is seeded by — so the result is reproducible and auditable.

          Per-match scoring

          • Win +3, Draw +1
          • Goal scored +1
          • Clean sheet +1
          • Beat a higher-pot team +3 (the upset bonus)

          Progression bonuses

          2026 has 48 teams, with 32 advancing to the knockouts (Round of 32). Bonuses are cumulative — a Finalist also collects every prior round's bonus.

          • Reach R32 (out of groups) +2
          • Reach R16 +4
          • Reach QF +6
          • Reach SF +10
          • Reach Final +15
          • Win it all +25

          The Cage multiplier

          At draw time, each team is paired to a Nicolas Cage film. The film's Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer becomes a permanent multiplier on the team's match points: 0.5 + rt/100, so a 0% film gives ×0.5 and a 100% film gives ×1.5. Progression bonuses are not multiplied.

          To stop anyone winning the lottery: film-pots are inversely paired with team-pots. The strongest teams (Pot 1) draw from the worst films (low multipliers); the weakest teams (Pot 4) draw from the best films (high multipliers). Every player's multiplier budget ends up roughly equal — but which film lands on Argentina is part of the draw drama.

          Knockout matches

          Goals scored in extra time count. Penalty-shootout tallies do not — the shootout decides who advances, but only goals in normal time and AET feed the per-match scoring. The advancing team is treated as the winner (gets the +3 win bonus and any applicable upset bonus).

          Tiebreakers

          Total points → total goals scored by your teams.
